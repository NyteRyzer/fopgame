## The Fairly OddParents: Magic Words!

A fan ROM hack of **Super Scribblenauts (USA)**: play as Timmy Turner and wish for anything.

### How to play

**Easiest:** on the [website](https://nyteryzer.github.io/fopgame/#get-the-rom), pick your ROM and the playable `.nds` downloads right away.

Or patch it yourself:

1. Download **`fop-magic-words.bps`** below.
2. Open [ROM Patcher JS](https://www.marcrobledo.com/RomPatcher.js/) (or Floating IPS).
3. Select your own dump of **Super Scribblenauts (USA)** (`BH2E`, CRC32 `69F707EA`) and the patch.
4. Save the patched `.nds` and play it in melonDS, DeSmuME, DraStic or on a flashcart.

### In this release

- Timmy Turner replaces Maxwell; Cosmo and Wanda are redrawn godparents
- New FOP title screen, pink/lavender/green UI and HUD
- All 237 level objectives rewritten as Fairly OddParents story missions
- The Wish map blueprint shows Timmy, Cosmo and Wanda
- ~85 new words: COSMO, WANDA, VICKY, CROCKER, JORGEN, CRIMSON CHIN, DA RULES, MAGIC MUFFIN...

The ROM itself is not distributed here: you need your own copy of the game.
