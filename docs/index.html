The FairlyOddParentsMagic Words!

A fan ROM hack of Super Scribblenauts for the Nintendo DS. Play as Timmy Turner, write down any word and Cosmo and Wanda make the wish come true.

What's inside

  • Timmy TurnerA hand-drawn Timmy replaces Maxwell: pink cap, buck teeth and all.
  • Cosmo & WandaWrite their names and Timmy's godparents poof in.
  • 237 story missionsVicky's traps, Crocker's pop quizzes, Jorgen's boot camp, Anti-Fairies and more.
  • ~85 new wordsVICKY, CROCKER, JORGEN, CRIMSON CHIN, DA RULES, MAGIC MUFFIN, WISH-O-METER...
  • FOP lookNew logo, gingham title, pink buttons, lavender panels, and Wishes instead of Starites.

How to play

  1. Download fop-magic-words.bps from the latest release.
  2. Open ROM Patcher JS in your browser (or use Floating IPS).
  3. Choose your own dump of Super Scribblenauts (USA) (BH2E, CRC32 69F707EA) and the patch, then save.
  4. Play the patched .nds in melonDS, DeSmuME, DraStic or on a flashcart.

The ROM is not distributed here: you need your own copy of the game.
